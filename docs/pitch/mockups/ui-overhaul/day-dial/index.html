Day Dial

The day is a 24-hour dial. Every entry lands as a coloured bead at the time it happened, so a sparse day is simply a quiet clock, and the journal starts showing when things happen, not only how much.

Every phone is live: tap, hold, drag and swipe inside it. The clock runs from 21:10 on Thursday 5 November 2026. All habits and entries are made up; units are the app's existing ones.
