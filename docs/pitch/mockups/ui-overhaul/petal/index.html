Petal for Tracker

The icon becomes the chart. Each habit owns a coloured petal on the icon's paper, so every day draws its own version of the app icon, and a quiet day reads as a small flower rather than a collapsed one. Every screen below is live: tap, hold, swipe and drag.

The system

Paper, ink and the icon's five hues. Fills carry identity; text stays in ink, or in a habit's darker ink variant on paper. Numbers are always monospaced so they can roll without shifting.
