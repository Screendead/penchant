What finished overnight

  • Insights is three sections again. Lasting changes of level sit behind More in "What it costs". A habit that rose as another fell is stated on the card for the fall. A heavy-day recovery finding joins the "N or more" card for the same habit and measure, and says which baseline each number uses ("31 min less than your usual that night").
  • Pairs of habits. The card now says two habits "tend to come together", and a pair is dropped when both simply follow waking. In test journals built that way, false pairs went from 100% to 0.3%; real pairs are found as often as before.
  • Health data on the device is encrypted with your journal key, as you asked: the daily Health numbers and the heart-rate samples Insights keeps between launches. The old unencrypted file is deleted at launch. Sign-out and account deletion remove all of it.
  • Settings › Account Settings › Recovery key has a new line saying where this device keeps its journal key. It only reads; it changes nothing.
  • Quit on the Mac hides the window at once and waits up to 3 seconds for unsynced entries before exiting.
  • Mac widgets say "Saved. Open Penchant to sync" when they hold logs and the menu bar helper is not running.
  • Insights after a fresh launch shows the last report at once, marked "Updating…", while the new one is worked out. The saved report is encrypted with your journal key and removed at sign-out.
  • Version 0.2.0 is cut: the first tagged release, with release notes on GitHub.
