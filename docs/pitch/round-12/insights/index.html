Recommend 1A: three question sections

  • It is the question-led page you agreed: the sections are the questions, in the build order, so each engine lands in a section that already exists.
  • Answers that span habits (Evening snack up since Wine went down) sit naturally under “How cutting down is going”; in 1C they have no home.
  • No cross-question ranking score to invent and tune, which 1B needs and which would put a bpm against a percentage.
  • “Not enough yet” is honest per question rather than a blank page: you can see that costs are answered while triggers still need weeks.

Recommend 2C: both, Today’s line opens Insights

  • Today is the screen you open every morning; Insights is not. A daily check that lives only in Insights (2B) is a check you rarely see.
  • Today stays one line, always sleeping heart rate (the measure the dose curve uses), and a tap for the rest. A fixed measure means no ranking a bpm against a percentage, the same score that counts against 1B. 2A crams three numbers in and still has no room for HRV or what you logged.
  • Half a day more than either alone, for one computation shown twice.
  • It shows only once Health is connected and only until noon, so Today is unchanged for anyone who hasn’t connected Health.
