What reads the note: Claude through our server, Apple's model on the device, or nothing

Today nothing reads a note. Notes are end-to-end encrypted: they are sealed on the device with your journal key, and neither our server nor anyone else can read them. Turning a note into tags needs a language model, and the choice of model decides whether your phone gets the feature and whether note text ever leaves the device.

Measured on this Mac today, Apple's on-device model, 12 made-up notes about drinking, smoking, vaping, cannabis and cocaine: all 12 answered, none refused, median 0.9 s, slowest 3.3 s. About half were tagged well. The rest carried a wrong tag.

Whether the app speaks up on a heavy day, and how

Today the app never comments on how much you logged. You have ruled three times in that direction: the daily reminder says only "Anything to add to today?", the experiment notification never names the habit, and a habit's line is a mark with no alert past it. You now want a prompt on heavy days asking what was unusual. That is arithmetic, not AI: when a log lands, the app compares the day's total with your own past days.

I would define heavy as a day in the top tenth of your last 90 days for that habit, once it has 30 days of history, instead of a number of standard deviations: daily counts are lopsided, and a few big days stretch the standard deviation until nothing counts as heavy.

Recommended A quiet notification that evening, off by default

At 21:00 on a heavy day: "Anything different about today?" It names no habit and no amount, so it is safe on a lock screen. Tapping opens the note panel for the day. One a day at most.

Risk: on a late-night heavy day it arrives before the heaviest part. A second check the next morning covers that.

Build: about 1 day.

Where a whole day's text is kept

This matters only if Call 2 is not "nothing". Today a note belongs to one entry and holds one line of up to 500 characters. A heavy day is many entries, and "what was unusual about today" belongs to none of them.

Recommended A new sealed day note, one per day

A new kind of encrypted document: the date, the text, its confirmed tags. It appears on that day's dial, in the export, and in Insights as a property of the day.

Cost: a change to the database rules, which deploy to production automatically when merged, and about 2 days. Older app versions ignore day notes.

Risk: low. Nothing existing changes shape.

Put it on the day's last entry

The text becomes that entry's note. No new data, no rules change, about half a day.

Risk: deleting or moving that entry takes the day's text with it, it overwrites any note already there, and Insights would credit the tags to one drink, not the day.

If you do nothing: I use the day note if Call 2 goes ahead.
