A night out on the Lock Screen

What the Live Activity looks like on an iPhone 13 Pro Max (no Dynamic Island). Drawn from ios/PenchantWidgets/NightWidget.swift; the real thing can only be seen on a device.

Default: the habit is not named

Name and colour shown (chosen when starting)

After it ends (stale date passed)

Layout, exactly

  • One row, 18 pt side padding, 14 pt top and bottom, on the app's paper colour (light) or ink (dark), following the phone's appearance.
  • Left: a 12 pt mark (an ink ring by default; the habit's colour if chosen), then the label in Red Hat Display Black 18 ("Tonight" or the habit's name) over "since 19:20" in Red Hat Text 13, muted. The time follows the phone's 12/24-hour setting.
  • Right: the count in Red Hat Mono SemiBold 36 (ink, or the habit's text colour if chosen), rolling to the new number, then a 52 pt round "+1" button (surface colour, hairline border).
  • Once the end time passes the button goes and the line reads "Ended · from 19:20". iOS keeps it on the Lock Screen up to 4 hours more unless swiped away.
  • Dynamic Island (other phones): the mark and the count compact; label, count and +1 expanded.
