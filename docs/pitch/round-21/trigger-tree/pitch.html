A fuller list of tags for "what set it off"

Today the app offers 40 tags in 7 areas. Reading 12 trial notes into tags showed the gap: an argument about money could only be filed under Work, because "Hard conversation" exists nowhere else. Most of the list is like that: a tag sits in one area and means less than its words.

Below is a proposed list of tags in 11 areas. It keeps every existing tag, because entries already saved point at them, and adds the rest. Four areas are new: what happened between people, something that happened, what you were doing, and what it was for. That last one records the purpose (to relax, to cope, to sleep), which is a different question from the cause and the one most worth comparing across habits.

Tap any new tag to strike it out, then copy your version at the bottom and paste it to me. Existing tags can't be removed, only renamed.

Adopt the fuller list, and how much of it

What changes for you: the tag picker goes from 7 tiles of about 6 tags to 11 tiles of about 15. That is too many to scan, so the picker gains a search box, and the note reader matters more, since it finds the tag for you. Trends and Insights group by area, so the four new areas appear there as their own groups. A tag's identity is permanent once shipped: later it can be renamed or hidden, never deleted or moved to another area.
